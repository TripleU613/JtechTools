# Changelog

What changed for forums running Jtech Tools. Newest first.

## Unreleased

**New**

- Moderator tools: slow mode for one user. From a user's admin page, staff can let them post one reply or new topic every so often (1 minute to a week apart), for a day, a week, a month, until a date or until lifted, with a reason. The user gets a message saying so, and their composer says how often they can post and how long until the next one; a post that's too soon is turned away with how long to wait, on the full site and in Dumbcourse. Private messages and edits aren't limited, and a post waiting for approval holds them until it's reviewed. Staff can't be put in slow mode. Settings: `mod_user_slow_mode_enabled`, `mod_user_slow_mode_moderators`.
- Mod elections: the results post shows the whole count, so anyone can see it was honest: everyone's first-choice votes and share as a bar chart, what each stage did, where everyone finished, how ties were settled, and every stage in numbers. Never who ranked whom. Elections already published get it too: their results post is updated in place without bumping the topic, or added if there's none.
- JTech theme: advanced search in the command menu (⌘K / Ctrl+K): Open advanced search goes to the search page with its filters open, without searching first. ⌘⇧K / Ctrl+Shift+K goes there from anywhere outside a text field ([#353](https://github.com/TripleU613/JtechTools/issues/353)).
- Mod elections: candidates can change their statement or drop out during Vote Week too, on the full site and in Dumbcourse. Someone who drops out in Vote Week can't come back on the ballot, and votes for them go to each voter's next choice. Once Vote Week is over, both are fixed.
- REQ-PM: WhatsApp takes a username as well as a number, as Telegram and Signal do. A number still gets the Chat link; a username is shown with a copy button, on the full site and in Dumbcourse.
- JTech theme and Dumbcourse: reactions roll out in a row. With the Reactions plugin, the heart still likes a post when clicked, and pointing at it rolls the reactions out sideways in the post's menu, pushing what's beside it along, instead of a box over the post; a row that doesn't fit scrolls (the mouse wheel scrolls it too). On phones a button beside the heart opens the row with a tap, the menu's other buttons but Reply step aside while it's open, and it fades at the edge where there are more to swipe to. Dumbcourse on a touch screen does the same in a post's footer, from the smile beside the like. Room for more than six reactions without the picker taking over the post. Setting: `reaction_rollout`.
- JTech theme: a fuller footer. An Account column of its own (Log in and Sign up, or your profile, messages, bookmarks and preferences), Back to top beside the copyright, small print between them (setting `footer_notice`, followed by a Terms link: the AI and scraping notice, shorter and in the footer instead of a band above it), and the default links in four columns: Forum (Latest, Top, Categories, Tags, Search), Community (Members, Groups, Badges, Guidelines, About), JTech (Homepage, Market, eGate filter, Dumbcourse, Contact) and Legal.
- Disteleplus: deleting works both ways. A message deleted on Telegram is deleted in the forum's chat too (within five minutes; an hour for older messages, up to a week), once a private check channel is set in `disteleplus_deletion_check_chat_id`: Telegram never tells a bot about deletions, so the bot checks. And a message written on Telegram can now be deleted from the forum by staff (or its matched author), which deletes it on Telegram as well; before, the forum couldn't delete it and the Telegram side kept it.
- JTech theme: the Discourse palette is stock Discourse, not only its colours. Picked, the forum takes Discourse's look: its topic lists (rows, not the theme's cards) under its row of tabs and Who's Online's own row, its shades instead of the flattened black and white (solid lines, its hover and selection tints), colour where Discourse has it (category icons and badges, letter avatars, the timeline, the online ring), 4px corners, round avatars, a solid header, uppercase sidebar headings, and Font Awesome icons instead of Lucide. Its dark version is on JTech Dim's soft greys, with Discourse's blue and a top bar darker than the page; JTech Dim itself goes, and whoever had picked it moves to Discourse Dark.
- JTech theme: a Dismiss all button under a topic's New & Unread Topics, beside "There are 37 unread topics remaining". It marks every new and unread topic read in one go (after asking), as Dismiss does on /unread and /new; before, each one had to be opened to go away.

- Mod elections: the forum elects its moderators. Every January, April, July and October (by default) nominations open for a week: sitting moderators are on the ballot already, and members at trust level 2 or higher with a 90-day-old account and a clean record can run with a short statement; they get a notification saying they can (`mod_elections_invite_candidates`). If nominations close with no more candidates than seats there's nothing to vote on, so they stay open three more days, once (`mod_elections_nomination_extension_days`); if it's still so, there's no Vote Week and the candidates are elected unopposed when an admin publishes. Otherwise, in Vote Week voters rank the candidates on `/elections` (or in Dumbcourse, with the keypad), and a vote counts as much as the voter's trust level when nominations opened. The count is single transferable vote, published stage by stage, with every tie and how it was settled. An admin checks for ballots cast from one address, previews the count and publishes it, which makes the winners moderators and takes moderator rights from the sitting moderators who weren't elected; publishing waits while a winner can't take the seat. Nobody sees who ranked whom, admins included, and once the results are out no ballot says whose it was; each voter still sees whether theirs was counted. The page moves on by itself when a step changes, counts down, and tells voters their ballot was counted; on a computer candidates can be dragged into place, and screen readers hear each change. Staff hear on Telegram (through Disteleplus) when voting closes and who won. The JTech theme styles it in light, dark and Dim. Former moderators keep trust level 4, locked, until their second lost re-election. A seat left mid-term is filled by counting the same ballots again. An admin can change an election under way, its seats and the dates still ahead, until voting closes. Admins never run or vote. Off by default: `mod_elections_enabled`. See [docs/features/mod-elections.md](docs/features/mod-elections.md).

## 0.5.0 — September 2026

A pass over every module to fix bugs, close permission holes and hand work back to core where core already does it.

**Check after upgrading**

- Moderators now manage categories through core's `moderators_manage_categories`. It's switched on for you if the old module grant was on, and it now covers only categories a moderator can see.
- Gravatar is switched off site-wide (`automatically_download_gravatars`, `gravatar_enabled`) if the old Username avatar module was on.
- Category moderators (Mini-mod) can no longer delete categories, and need `mini_mod_manage_all_categories` to create top-level ones.

**Permissions**

- Mini-mod:
  - Category moderators could make private categories public, hand moderation to any group, and reach categories they couldn't see. All closed.
  - Reopen restrictions now also cover "open" topic timers.
- Moderator tools:
  - Moderators could edit, re-permission or delete admin-only categories. Closed.
  - Every endpoint checks topic visibility.
  - Staff alerts and the notes feed reach only staff who can see the topic. A deleted PM post used to reach every moderator.
  - Checklists no longer leak hidden topics.
  - Whisper conversions are logged in staff actions.

**Fixed**

- Dislike: notification suppression and the audit trail never ran. Likes in restricted categories now never notify, reactions included, and the like totals survive the hourly directory refresh.
- Moderator tools:
  - Non-staff topic lists (/top, /hot, custom sort orders) were re-sorted by bump date. That's removed.
  - Whisper targets can mark a trailing whisper read.
  - Topic pages no longer run one query per post for whisper checks.
  - Whisper recipients get one notification, not two.
  - Concurrent note edits no longer overwrite each other.
- Smart search:
  - Retries keep the search's filters.
  - Only the first page is expanded.
  - Retries no longer fill the search log.
  - A topic isn't listed twice.
  - Extra synonyms apply on every server process.
  - The dictionary was pruned of false and everyday-word synonyms.
- Pop-ups:
  - quiet during Do Not Disturb;
  - usable from the keyboard, with a close button;
  - the preference only shows on your own account page;
  - likes no longer show your own avatar as the actor's.
- Another SMTP: group inbox mail keeps its own server, and the dashboard warns about a relay with no address.
- Translator tweaks: removed the globe-hiding tweak that left old posts untranslatable.

**Removed**

- Username avatar, replaced by core settings. The reset script no longer wipes the system user's and bots' avatars.
